Momos

A variety of choices, from our steamed to our bhaktapur jhol, come in your preferred spice level.

  • Steamed Momo

    Soft steamed dumplings filled with chicken or vegetables, enhanced with Himalayan spices and served warm with our tomato-based house dipping sauce.

  • Fried Momo

    Crispy fried dumplings filled with chicken or vegetables, enhanced with Himalayan spices and served with our tomato-based house dipping sauce.

  • Sadheko Momo

    Steamed momos tossed with fresh herbs, green onions, ginger, and our house tomato-based sauce.

  • Crispy Saucy Momo

    Crispy fried momos glazed in our sweet and savory tomato-based sauce, available mild, medium, or spicy.

  • Bhaktapur Jhol Momo

    Steamed momos served in a cold, tangy, tomato-based, soybean and sesame enhanced jhol with fresh herbs and Himalayan spices. Perfect chill for warm Texas weather.

  • Chilly Momo

    Golden-fried momos tossed in our signature house chili glaze with fresh onions, sweet bell peppers, and jalapeno - crunchy, sweet, and little spice in every bite.

  • Tato Jhol Momo

    Steamed momos served in a warm, rich, savory tomato jhol (soup) with fresh herbs and Himalayan spices - comforting, aromatic, and soothing.
