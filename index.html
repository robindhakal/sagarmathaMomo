Momos

A variety of choices, from our steamed to our bhaktapur jhol, come in your preferred spice level.

  • Sadheko Momo

    Steamed momo tossed with fresh cut herbs and seasonings including ginger, green onions, and house special tomato based sauce.

  • Steamed Momo

    Steamed momo warmed to perfection and served with our house dipping sauce.

  • Fried Momo

    Fried momo with a golden outer layer and a savory filling that pairs well with our house sauce.

  • Bhaktapur Jhol Momo

    Steamed momo served in a cold jhol soup made with soyabean and spices, giving it a chilled, tangy flavor.

  • Tato Jhol Momo

    Steamed momo served in tomato based soup with bright, savory finish.

  • Chilly Momo

    Fried momo sauteed with onions, peppers, and house chili sauce for a bold and flavorful finish.
